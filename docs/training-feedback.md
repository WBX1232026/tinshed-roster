# Training and Feedback

> Owner: Yao Tingran (Business/Requirements Lead). Records the client training session and its feedback.

## 1. Training session

- **Audience:** Judith Ambrose (President/Producer), Bec Tanaka (Volunteer coordinator), Ravi Chandrasekaran (Technical director).
- **Format:** walk-through of the three core workflows, followed by a hands-on session where Bec built a roster end-to-end.
- **Duration:** ~30 minutes.

### Covered

1. Creating and updating volunteers.
2. Creating a production, its performances and a crew call.
3. Assigning volunteers and understanding the one-role rule.
4. Reading the roster gap view and the personal roster view.

## 2. Feedback received

| Persona | Feedback | Action taken |
|---|---|---|
| Bec Tanaka | "The gap view is exactly what I needed — I can finally see the holes before I start phoning people." | No change; confirmed the view matches her workflow. |
| Judith Ambrose | "I still keep the wall roster for the committee — is this the source of truth?" | Recorded as an open question; the software is positioned as the source of truth, the wall roster a display. |
| Ravi Chandrasekaran | "The most valuable thing would be who is actually trained on lights and sound." | Queued as PBI-1 (top of the next-sprint backlog). |
| Bec Tanaka | "Will it warn me if I roster someone who said they are away?" | Queued as PBI-2 (unavailable dates). |

## 3. Outcome

All three personas were able to complete the core scenarios unaided. The software is accepted for the sprint scope; the deferred items are captured in the product backlog with agreed priorities.
